# v1.0.20: Tissue selection and regional expression estimates

This version adds the revised manuscript's two figures, Table 1 and nine supporting tables. The new summaries are generated from the released per-map real-resource effects and full simulation tables; Figure 2 additionally uses the included registered Valdeolivas H&E image and saved spot membership for one section. The generation commands are in `README.md`.

Four of 17 eligible sections had a morphology-defined contrast meeting the historical support rule. Among 117 evaluable stromal-retention maps in those sections, 41 retained support: 15/41 reference maps and 26/76 alternatives. These are repeated map selections within four patients, not patient-level rates. The real analysis concerns subsets of fixed morphology-defined bands and does not demonstrate local histological-boundary movement or a biological conclusion reversal.

The simulation's reference and alternative maps are deterministic displacements of the known segmentation; the identity control is reported separately. Conditional support loss under constructed shifts is not a null-hypothesis false-positive rate or evidence of how a clustering algorithm behaves in colorectal cancer tissue. Previous descriptions that used the simulation to rank map agreement against regional fidelity should be read in that narrower light.

The archive permits regeneration of the current manuscript figures and tables from released derived inputs. A complete raw-expression-to-map replay still requires the source-compatible registered spot inputs and saved maps described in the manifest; those are not included in this release.
